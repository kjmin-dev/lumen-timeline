# Clip speed and time stretch

**Status.** Delivered in one pull request, PR S.

**User outcome.** In a dubbing studio, fit a line to its picture by dragging the clip's edge in stretch mode, or by typing a speed. The line keeps its words and its voice: it plays faster or slower at its own pitch, and trims, splits, takes, undo, redo and a shared session all keep working on it. Without a stretch engine the same project plays the clip as varispeed. [Design](../design.md#editing-commands) · [Milestone 3](../../ROADMAP.md#milestones)

**Starting point.** After #61. A clip maps timeline to content one to one: `contentTime = contentIn + (projectTime - start)`. Edge drags only trim, and the design lists pitch-preserving time stretch among the extensions after the first release. A dubbing host had no way to change a line's speed.

## Model

- **Rate.** A clip's optional `rate` is content ticks per timeline tick, from `MIN_CLIP_RATE` (0.25) to `MAX_CLIP_RATE` (4). The clip covers `duration × rate` of content. A snapshot omits a rate of 1, so every existing project and every edit of a unit-speed clip is unchanged.
- **Content stays media time.** The rate applies between timeline and content, not between content and source. `contentIn`, `contentBounds`, a take's `sourceAtContentZero` and a split's `atContent` therefore name the same media at every speed. The alternative, scaling the content axis with the rate, keeps every placement integer but makes `contentBounds` and `atContent` change meaning when a clip is retimed: a split recorded before another editor's retime would replay at different media.
- **Rounding.** `contentAt(clip, tick) = contentIn + round((tick - start) × rate)`, half away from zero, is the one mapping the editor, the planner and the view use. At rate 1 it is exact. Elsewhere a left trim can move the content end by one tick, 1/5880 of a 48 kHz sample, which no sample boundary can show.

## Core

- **Command.** `retimeClip({ clipId, rate | duration, anchor? })` keeps the content and changes the timeline length. A new `rate` sets the length; a new `duration` sets the rate, `rate × duration / newDuration`, which settles to exactly 1 within rounding. `anchor: "end"` keeps the end. Preview accepts it, so a drag shows it first. New code `invalid-rate`.
- **Other edits.** Trims move content by the delta at the rate. A split gives both halves the rate, and the right half starts at exactly `atContent`. A recorded split by `atContent` therefore replays at the same media after a concurrent retime.
- **History.** Replay merges a placement at rate 1 by origin and content edges, as before. A placement with a rate, before, after or now, is one value: start, duration, content offset and rate. Undoing a retime after a move of the same clip conflicts instead of merging positions that round against each other. `clipFieldChanges` lists `rate` under `fields`.
- **Planning.** `sourceWindow` and `planAudio` map both ends of a window through `contentAt`, and availability bounds through the rate. `AudioSpan.rate` carries the clip's rate. `SourceWindow` gains `sourceEnd`.

## Media

- **Varispeed.** Without an engine, a voice plays its source at `rate` times the transport speed from the clip's source position. `scheduleWindow` takes the clip rate apart from the transport speed, so output times stay on the timeline.
- **Stretch engines.** `MediaOptions.stretch(request, signal)` renders the audible source range to the audible timeline length. The voice plays the rendering from its audible start at the timeline's pace and keeps its seam booking. A failed or short rendering is reported to `onError` and falls back to varispeed. A buffer that needs no stretching takes exactly the promise turns it took before.
- **Signalsmith Stretch.** `@lumen-timeline/media/signalsmith` renders with [Signalsmith Stretch](https://signalsmith-audio.co.uk/code/stretch/) 1.3.2, MIT, an optional peer dependency loaded on first use. Each rendering runs the library's AudioWorklet in an `OfflineAudioContext` with a 0.1 s preroll and a quarter second of context on each side. A byte-budgeted cache keyed by source buffer, range and length serves seeks, replays and other transports, and a concurrency limit skips renderings nobody waits for any more.

## View

- **Edge mode.** `edgeMode: "trim" | "stretch"`, a **Stretch** switch (`chrome.stretchToggle`), R on the clip surface and `events.onEdgeModeChange`. A stretch drag dispatches a retime by duration anchored at the other edge, snaps like a trim, and is held to the rate range and a start at or after zero. `update` applies `edgeMode` only when the host's value changes.
- **Display.** A clip with a rate shows it after its name, as `125%`. Its waveform maps columns through `contentAt`, so it shows the content the clip plays.
- **Edit form.** A Speed operation takes a percentage, starting from the selected clip's own.
- **Wrappers.** React, Svelte, SolidJS and Vue forward `edgeMode` like every other option.
